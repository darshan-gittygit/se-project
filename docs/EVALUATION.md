## How each metric is computed

- **Extraction precision / recall / F1:** gold and system requirements are paired one-to-one when every keyword group of the gold requirement has a word-start match in the system requirement's text. Precision = pairs / system requirements (n/a when the system returned none); recall = pairs / gold requirements.
- **Completeness:** share of the gold's requirement categories with at least one extracted requirement. Also shown: category agreement (paired requirements whose system categories include the gold category) and record completeness (system records with acceptance criteria, justification, categories and a quote or assumption).
- **Ambiguity detection:** planted vague phrases named by an ambiguity or untestable flag (in its reason or in a flagged requirement's text) over all planted phrases. Flags that match nothing planted are counted separately; they may be real findings.
- **Conflict detection:** planted conflicts for which a conflict flag links requirements paired with both sides, or whose reason matches the conflict's keyword groups, over all planted conflicts.
- **Regulatory-control coverage:** (gold requirement, expected knowledge-base source) pairs present in the paired system requirement's compliance mapping, over all expected pairs.
- **Hallucination rate:** requirements whose quote is not in the (masked) input, or that have neither a quote nor an assumption, over all requirements. The app already rejects such records, so this mainly confirms the guard.
- **Citation correctness:** compliance mappings whose evidence quote occurs in that source's knowledge-base text and whose source is one the gold expects for the requirement, over the mappings of paired requirements.
- **SDLC accuracy:** top-1 = the recommended model is the gold choice; top-3 = the gold choice is in the system's top three; acceptable = the recommendation is the gold choice or one of its listed alternatives.
- **Human correction rate:** (modify + reject) over all review decisions (accept, reject, modify, regenerate) recorded for the run in the app database; n/a until someone reviews the run in the UI.
- **Traceability coverage:** requirements with a backward link (quote or assumption), a forward link (a story, use case, process workflow, data or interface requirement) and acceptance criteria, over all requirements.
- **Processing time:** wall-clock seconds of the `/agents/pipeline` request, including retries and back-off.

## Limitations

- **Small sample:** six case studies, one run each; differences of one requirement move a rate by 6-7 points. No confidence intervals are claimed.
- **One labeller:** the gold, the planted problems and the manual baseline were written by the same person, so they share that person's view of the scenarios; there is no inter-rater agreement figure. The gold was frozen before the runs (its hash is stored with each result) to avoid tuning it to the output.
- **Keyword matching:** a consistent but approximate stand-in for human judgement: unlisted synonyms count as misses, broad keywords can over-match, and merged requirements pair with only one gold item.
- **Planted problems:** detection rates are recall over planted items only; extra flags are reported but not judged, so there is no false-positive rate.
- **Synthetic scenarios:** realistic but written for this evaluation, not real or anonymised bank documents.
- **Free-tier models:** results depend on whichever Gemini model answered (listed per run), on rate limits and retries (which inflate processing time), and on run-to-run variation in trait ratings.
- **Knowledge base:** a small set of paraphrased principles; insurance and regulatory reporting have no domain-specific source, so their regulatory coverage tests mostly the generic baseline.
- **Satisfaction:** a handful of respondents (classmates or the author) is not a stakeholder survey.
